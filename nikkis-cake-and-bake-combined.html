Our Story

16 Years of Sweet Perfection.

Nikki's Cake & Bake is a home bakery run by a passionate baker based in Kaspate Wasti, Wakad, crafting premium, customised cakes for over 16 years. Every treat is handcrafted, 100% eggless, and made to the highest hygiene standards — because at Nikki's, you can taste the quality.

We use only pure butter and milk — no palm oil, no shortcuts. From elegant semi-fondant finishes to unique theme designs, every order is baked fresh for that signature homemade taste.

How Booking Works

  • 1. Pick a date & slot → Slots fill on a first-come basis, so a booked slot is immediately marked unavailable to everyone.
  • 2. Tell us the brief → Flavour, size, theme, and occasion.
  • 3. Instant confirmation → Your request lands directly in our WhatsApp with a reference ID — nothing gets lost in a form that "goes nowhere."
  • 4. We call you back → Usually within a couple of hours during business hours (10 AM – 8 PM).

By booking, your request is sent directly to +91 91300 93430 on WhatsApp. Slot availability is checked live against our booking system, so it's accurate across every device in real time.
