Our Story

Handcrafted Happiness, One Layer at a Time.

Nikki's Cake & Bake began with a simple belief — that every celebration deserves a cake made with real ingredients, real craft, and real care. Based in Wakad, Pune, we bake everything fresh to order: no shortcuts, no store-bought mixes, just butter, love and precision.

From towering wedding cakes to whimsical birthday designs and everyday indulgences like cupcakes and brownies, every order is treated like the most important one of the day — because to the person receiving it, it is.

How Booking Works

  • 1. Pick a date & slot → Slots fill on a first-come basis, so a booked slot is immediately marked unavailable to everyone.
  • 2. Tell us the brief → Flavour, size, theme, and occasion.
  • 3. Instant confirmation → Your request lands directly in our WhatsApp with a reference ID — nothing gets lost in a form that "goes nowhere."
  • 4. We call you back → Usually within a couple of hours during business hours (10 AM – 8 PM).

By booking, your request is sent directly to +91 91300 93430 on WhatsApp. This slot list runs locally in your browser for instant availability checks — full cross-device sync can be added with a Supabase backend (already part of the Tako Designs stack) if Nikki's team wants a shared live dashboard next.

Automated Front Desk

What's automated today vs. what's a real phone/telephony build

An AI that actually answers phone calls, reads emails, and replies to SMS needs telephony + email infrastructure behind the website (e.g. Twilio for calls/SMS, a connected inbox, and an automation layer) — it can't run inside a static webpage. Here's the straight status, not a demo that looks live but isn't:

Nikki's team already has an n8n automation instance, so the fastest real path is: missed call → auto SMS/WhatsApp text-back with booking link, new email → AI-drafted reply for approval, and a Claude-powered WhatsApp responder for FAQs.
